Demolition & Removal

Controlled removal of small structures, concrete, paving and unwanted material.

Old concrete, pavers and small structures often need to go before new work can start. We break out and remove them in a controlled way, protecting what’s staying.

Compact machinery means we can do this in backyards and tight spaces without bringing in large equipment.

When you might need it

  • An old driveway, path or slab is being replaced
  • Pavers or hardstand need lifting
  • A small structure needs removing before new work

What’s included

  • Concrete breakout and removal
  • Paver and hardstand removal
  • Small structure demolition
  • Old path and driveway removal
  • Garden edging and wall removal
  • Loading and disposal of rubble
