FAQ

Fair questions. Straight answers.

The calls

Will callers know it's AI?

It sounds natural and talks like your office would. If someone asks, it's honest that it's your virtual receptionist. Most callers just care that someone picked up.

What happens with emergencies?

You decide what counts as urgent. Urgent calls are flagged and texted to you straight away so you can call back fast.

What if a caller wants to talk to me?

It takes their details and texts you so you can call them back. Can it transfer live calls? Confirm.

Does it work after hours?

Yes. It can answer 24/7, or only when you choose.
