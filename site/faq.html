The calls

Will callers know it's AI?

It sounds natural and friendly, and it's set up to talk like your office would. If someone asks, it's honest that it's your virtual receptionist. Most callers just care that someone picked up and sorted them out.

What does it sound like?

The best way to find out is to ring it: (02) XXXX XXXX. You can also watch sample calls.

What happens with emergencies?

You decide what counts as urgent, like a burst pipe, no power or an unsafe site. Urgent calls get flagged and texted to you straight away, so you can call back fast.

What if a caller wants to talk to me?

It takes their details and what it's about, and texts you so you can call them back. Can it transfer live calls? Confirm.

Does it work after hours and on weekends?

Yes. It can answer 24/7, or only when you choose, like missed calls during the day and everything after 6pm.

What happens if someone hangs up?

They get a text in your business name with a short enquiry form. Plenty of people won't talk to someone new but will happily fill in a form.
