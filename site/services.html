FAQs

Common questions

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes. We provide a free on-site consultation and a detailed, itemised quote with no obligation.

How long does a typical project take?

Smaller jobs such as turf or a garden bed can take 1–3 days. Full backyard renovations typically take 2–6 weeks depending on size and complexity. We'll give you a clear timeline with your quote.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — we're fully licensed and carry public liability insurance. All work is backed by our workmanship guarantee.

Can you work with my existing design or builder?

Absolutely. We regularly build from architect and designer plans, and partner with builders for new-home landscaping.

Do you service commercial properties?

Yes. We provide construction and ongoing maintenance for strata, offices, retail and builder developments.
