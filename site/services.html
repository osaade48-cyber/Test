Services

What TradeBooked does for you.

Five jobs it takes off your plate. Each one shows when it happens and the message that gets sent.

Answers your missed calls

Picks up when you can't, finds out what the job is, books a time and takes messages.

When: you don't answer within a few rings

Text to you

New job booked
Sarah, Castle Hill. Retaining wall. Site visit Thu 7:30am.

Texts people who hang up

If a caller hangs up without talking, they get a text with a short enquiry form, so you don't lose the lead.

When: a caller hangs up

Text to your caller

Sorry we missed your call! Tell us about your job here and we'll get back to you today.

Follows up your quotes

Checks in with customers after you've sent a quote, so it doesn't go cold. Replies come to you.

When: a few days after you quote

Text to your customer

Hi Sarah, just checking you got our quote for the retaining wall? Happy to answer any questions.

Asks for Google reviews

Sends happy customers a link to review you on Google. More reviews means more calls.

When: the job is finished

Text to your customer

Thanks for choosing us, Dave! Would you mind leaving a quick Google review? It really helps.

Reminds people to pay

Sends polite invoice reminders, so you get paid without chasing.

When: before and after the due date

Text to your customer

Hi Mark, friendly reminder that invoice #1042 for $3,850 is due this Friday. Thanks!
