Questions about Lead Engine

Is the ad spend included?

No. You pay Meta directly for your ads, at least $50 a day. That way you always see exactly what's being spent, and every dollar goes into ads.

Why is there a 3-month minimum?

Ads take a few weeks to learn who's most likely to book in your area. Three months gives them a fair go to bring in steady work.

How many leads will I get?

It depends on your trade, area and ad budget, so we can't promise a number. We'll talk through what's realistic for you on our chat. Add your Avera Earthworx results here if you can share them.
