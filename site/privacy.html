Who we are

TradeBooked (ABN XX XXX XXX XXX) provides AI receptionist, SMS and follow-up services to trade businesses in Australia. In this policy, "we" means TradeBooked.

What we collect

  • From our customers (tradies): name, business name, phone, email, billing details and the business information needed to set up your AI receptionist.
  • From people who call our customers: name, phone number, address or suburb, details of the job, and call recordings and transcripts.
  • From this website: what you enter in our forms, and basic usage data such as pages visited.

How we use it

  • To answer calls and pass enquiries, bookings and messages to the business that was called
  • To send texts on behalf of that business, such as enquiry forms, quote follow-ups, review requests and invoice reminders
  • To provide, support, bill for and improve our service
  • To reply to you if you contact us or book a chat

Who we share it with

Caller information goes to the trade business you called. We use trusted service providers for phone, SMS, AI processing, calendars and hosting. List providers and where they store data, including whether it's stored overseas. We don't sell personal information.

How long we keep it

e.g. call recordings and transcripts are kept for X days, then deleted.

Your choices

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. You can reply STOP to any text to opt out of further messages.

Contact and complaints

Email hello@tradebooked.com.au. If you're not happy with how we handle a complaint, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
