Questions about Booked Out

Is the ad spend included?

No. You pay Meta directly, at least $50 a day. That way you see exactly what's spent, and every dollar goes into ads.

Why is there a 3-month minimum?

Ads take a few weeks to learn who's most likely to book in your area. Three months gives them a fair go.

How many leads will I get?

It depends on your trade, area and budget, so we won't promise a number. We'll talk through what's realistic on our chat. Add your Avera Earthworx results here if you can share them.

What areas do you cover?

Your answer, e.g. "Anywhere in Australia. Ads target the suburbs you service."
