Questions about bundles

Can I combine bundles?

Yes, and you get 10% off the second one. Popular combos are Brand & Website with Get Found, and Booked Out with Get Found.

Is ad spend included?

No. For Booked Out you pay Meta directly, minimum $50 a day, so you see exactly what's spent.

Are there minimum terms?

Booked Out has a 3-month minimum and Get Found has 6 months, because ads and SEO take time to work. Never Miss a Job: your answer.
