Access we'll need

We never need your passwords. These steps give us our own access, and you can remove it any time.

Do you have a Google Business Profile? (your listing on Google Maps)

Add us as a manager on Google:

  1. Search your business name on Google while logged in, or go to business.google.com
  2. Open Business Profile settings → People and access
  3. Click Add, enter hello@tradebooked.com.au, choose Manager, then Invite
What's it built on?

Add us to WordPress:

  1. Log in to your site, go to Users → Add New
  2. Email: hello@tradebooked.com.au, Role: Editor, then Add New User
Do you have a Facebook business page?

Give us access in Meta Business Suite:

  1. Go to business.facebook.com → Settings → Partners
  2. Click Add partner → Give a partner access to your assets
  3. Enter our Business ID (we'll text it to you), then tick your Page and Ad account
