About Lotus 8 Group

A local landscaping team dedicated to creating outdoor spaces that last.

Our story

Built on craftsmanship and trust.

Lotus 8 Group was founded with a simple idea: clients deserve one reliable team that can take a landscape from concept to completion — and look after it afterwards.

Today we deliver complete landscape solutions across Sydney & surrounds, working with homeowners, builders, strata managers and commercial clients. Our crew combines design know-how with hands-on construction skills, so what we draw is exactly what we build.

The lotus represents growth and renewal; the 8 represents balance and prosperity. That's what we aim to bring to every property we touch.
