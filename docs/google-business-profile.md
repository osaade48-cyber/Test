# Google Business Profile: what's included per service

| Client buys | No Google profile? |
|---|---|
| **SEO** | Free: full setup **and** optimisation (covered by the SEO setup fee) |
| **CRM / follow-ups** | Free **basic** setup, so review requests work. Optimisation is the SEO upsell |
| **Website** | Free **basic** setup. Optimisation is the SEO upsell |
| **Google Ads** | Free **basic** setup (location and reviews show under the ads) |

**Basic setup** (about 30 minutes, one-off): create the profile with business name, category, service area (address hidden for home-based tradies), hours, phone and website. Add services, a description and a few photos. Guide the client through Google's verification video. Copy the review link into the client's GHL **Review Link** custom value.

**Optimisation** (SEO service, ongoing): all main and extra categories, every service listed, keyword-rich description, weekly posts and photos, Q&A, review strategy, consistent directory listings (NAP), ranking tracking.

**Exception:** fixing a messy existing profile (duplicates, suspension, wrong owner, old address) is charged separately, or covered by the setup fee for SEO clients.

Onboarding: if the client answers "No" to "Do you have a Google Business Profile?", the form collects their business name, area and hours for the basic setup.
