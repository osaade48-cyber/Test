Footings & Foundations

Accurate excavation for footings, foundations and structural preparation.

Footings carry the weight of everything built on top of them, so excavation has to match the plans: right position, width and depth.

We dig footings for builders, owner-builders and landscapers, including extensions and structures in backyards with limited access.

When you might need it

  • An extension, deck or granny flat needs footings
  • A retaining wall is going in
  • Your builder needs footings dug on a tight site

What’s included

  • Strip footing excavation
  • Pad and pier holes
  • Retaining wall footings
  • Extension and granny flat footings
  • Excavation to engineer’s plans
  • Spoil removal

Can you dig footings to engineering plans?

Yes. Provide the plans or set-out and we’ll excavate to the specified dimensions and depths.

Can you dig footings in a backyard with tight access?

Yes. Compact machinery lets us reach backyards and side areas that larger machines can’t.

Do you remove the soil from footings?

Yes, spoil can be loaded and taken away, or spread on site if suitable.
