Trenching

Precision trenching for services, utilities, drainage and site infrastructure.

Trenching needs to be accurate and controlled: the right width, depth and fall, without damaging what’s already in the ground or around it.

We dig trenches for plumbers, electricians, landscapers and homeowners, including along fence lines, beside houses and through narrow side passages.

When you might need it

  • A plumber or electrician needs a trench dug
  • New stormwater or ag lines are going in
  • Services need to run through a narrow side passage

What’s included

  • Service and utility trenches
  • Stormwater and drainage trenches
  • Conduit trenches
  • Trenching along fences and walls
  • Backfilling and compaction
  • Spoil removal

Can you dig trenches close to a house or fence?

Yes. Compact machinery and careful operation let us trench in tight spaces close to structures.

Do you locate existing underground services?

Existing services should be located before digging. We’ll talk through this on the site visit, including a Before You Dig Australia enquiry.

Can you backfill the trench afterwards?

Yes. Once the pipes or conduits are in, we can backfill and tidy the area.
