Landscaping Prep

Ground preparation for lawns, gardens, retaining walls and outdoor works.

Good landscaping depends on what’s underneath. We prepare the ground so turf, paving, garden beds and retaining walls go down on the right levels.

We work with landscapers and homeowners, often in established backyards where access is tight and the surroundings need protecting.

When you might need it

  • You’re laying new turf or paving
  • Garden beds or retaining walls are going in
  • An old yard needs stripping back before landscaping

What’s included

  • Excavation for paving and paths
  • Turf preparation and levelling
  • Garden bed shaping
  • Retaining wall excavation
  • Removal of old lawn and soil
  • Spoil removal
