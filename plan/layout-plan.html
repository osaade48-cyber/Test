The big idea

The whole site follows one missed call.

Most AI receptionist sites list features. This one tells a story every tradie has lived: the phone rings while you're in the trench, and that job goes to someone else. We show that call answered, then show the same call going wrong, then put a dollar figure on it. Plain words, big numbers, nothing to figure out.

A live call in the hero

A phone on screen gets a real-sounding call, the AI answers, books a site visit, and a text summary lands on the owner's lock screen. They see the product working in the first 10 seconds.

"Same call. Two endings."

A split screen, with the call nobody answered greyed out next to the call TradeBooked answered in orange. On a phone it's a switch you flick. This is where the loss hits home.

A calculator that turns it into money

Three sliders give one big orange number for what missed calls cost them a year, set right next to the cost of a plan. It makes the price feel small.

Look & feel

What will make it feel like nobody else's.

Design details

  • Hi-vis orange used like site safety gear
    Only on things that matter: money, actions, the AI's voice. Everything else is charcoal and warm concrete grey.
  • Real SMS and call screens instead of stock icons
    Every feature is shown as the actual message a tradie would see on their phone.
  • Motion with a purpose
    The phone call, the two endings and the counting numbers move. Nothing spins for the sake of it. Fully respects "reduce motion" settings.
  • Big, bold Poppins headlines, easy DM Sans body
    Short sentences. Reads easily in the ute at smoko.

How I'll build it

  • Fast on a 4G phone on site
    Plain HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript. No heavy frameworks. Aiming for under 2 seconds to load.
  • Found on Google
    Set up for searches like "AI receptionist for tradies" and "missed call answering Sydney".
  • Accessible
    Good contrast, keyboard friendly, and big tap targets for gloved or dusty thumbs.
  • Easy to host
    Works on Netlify, Vercel or any basic host pointed at tradebooked.com.au.
