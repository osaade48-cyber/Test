How it works

Set up in an afternoon. Answering from day one.

No new phone. No app to learn. Your number stays the same. Calls you can't get to go to your AI receptionist, and you get a text with what matters.

Step 1 · About 5 minutes

Keep your number. Forward the calls you miss.

We set up call forwarding on your mobile, so if you don't pick up after a few rings, the call goes to your AI receptionist. Answer when you can. It covers you when you can't.

  • Works with your existing mobile or landline
  • Choose when it picks up: missed calls, after hours, or both
  • Turn it off any time

Step 2 · We set it up with you

It answers like your office would.

On a quick call we go through your services, where you work, your hours and what counts as urgent. Your AI receptionist asks the questions you'd ask, in plain Aussie English.

  • Asks the right questions for your trade
  • Knows your service area and turns away jobs you don't do
  • Books into your calendar, only when you're free

Step 3 · Every call

You get a text with what matters.

No voicemails to dig through. Every call becomes a short text you can read at smoko. Hang-ups get a text with your enquiry form, so even they turn into leads.

  • Name, number, suburb and the job
  • Booked time, already in your calendar
  • Urgent calls texted straight away
