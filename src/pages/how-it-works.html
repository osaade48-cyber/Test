How it works

We find the jobs. Then we book them in.

Two parts working together: marketing that brings homeowners to you, and an AI receptionist that calls, answers and books every one of them.

Part 1 · Marketing

From ad to booked quote. On autopilot.

Here's what happens to every new lead, day or night.

  1. 1

    We run ads in your area

    Meta ads (Facebook and Instagram) shown to homeowners near you who need your kind of work.

    SponsoredNeed a retaining wall?Free quotes this week.Get a quote
  2. 2

    A homeowner asks for a quote

    They fill in a short form right inside Facebook or Instagram.

    NameJess TaylorSuburbKellyvilleJobPool dig
  3. 3

    Your AI calls them within a minute

    While they're still keen. It asks about the job and checks the details.

    Calling Jess…0:42
  4. 4

    It books the quote

    Straight into your calendar, at a time you're free.

    THU
    Quote: pool dig7:30am · Kellyville
  5. 5

    Confirmations and reminders

    They get a text to confirm, and a reminder the day before, so they're home when you arrive.

    You're booked for Thu 7:30am. See you then!

    Reminder: we'll see you tomorrow at 7:30am.

  6. 6

    Follow-ups if they go quiet

    No answer? It follows up by text and call, so leads don't slip away.

    Hi Jess, still keen on that pool dig? Reply YES and we'll lock in a time.

    YES

  7. 7

    You turn up and quote

    You get a text with everything you need. No chasing, no phone tag.

    ✓Ready to quote

Part 2 · AI receptionist

And when customers ring you directly.

You keep your number. When you can't pick up, here's what happens.

  1. 1

    A customer calls. You can't answer.

    You're on the tools, driving or with a client. After a few rings, the call goes to your AI receptionist.

  2. 2

    Your AI receptionist answers.

    It answers in your business name, asks what the job is and where, and books a time that suits you.

  3. 3

    You get a text with the details.

    Who called, what they need and when they're booked in. If they hung up, they get a text with an enquiry form.
