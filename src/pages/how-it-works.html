Getting started

From your first text to new jobs, in 4 steps.

No meetings, no jargon. Most of it happens over text while you're on the tools.

  1. 1

    Tell us what's going on

    Take the 1-minute quiz or text us. Too quiet, missing calls, can't be found on Google? Just say.

  2. 2

    We agree a plan

    We text you a plan and a price that fits your budget. Nothing starts until you're happy with it.

  3. 3

    We set it all up

    You fill in one short form. We build everything, test it and show you before it goes live. You'll get a clear timeline up front.

  4. 4

    Jobs start coming in

    New enquiries land on your phone, and every month you get a simple report showing what's working.

Quick questions

Do I need all three parts?

No. Pick what you need. Most tradies start with their biggest problem and add the rest later. Not sure? The 1-minute quiz will suggest a plan.

What do I actually have to do?

Answer a few questions in one short form, and give us access to things like your Google profile (we'll show you how, no passwords needed). We do the rest.

How long until it's live?

It depends on what you choose. We'll give you a clear timeline before anything starts.

Do I keep my phone number?

Yes. Customers ring you like normal. With the AI receptionist or missed-call text-back, the calls you can't answer get picked up for you.

Is there a contract?

Growth has a 3-month minimum and Visibility has 6 months, because ads and SEO take time to work. We'll confirm the terms for anything else when we text you.
