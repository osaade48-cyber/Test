Services

Answers the phone. Then chases everything else.

Missed calls are where you lose the most work, but they aren't the only place. Quotes go cold, reviews never get asked for and invoices sit unpaid. TradeBooked handles all of it by text, in your business name.

The big one

AI call answering

When you can't pick up, your AI receptionist does. It answers in your business name, works out what the caller needs, books a site visit or takes a message, and texts you the lot.

  • Answers missed and after-hours calls, 24/7
  • Asks the questions you'd ask for your trade
  • Books site visits into your calendar
  • Flags urgent jobs and texts you straight away

In all plans. Booking site visits is in Growth and Full system.

  • Answers come straight to you by text
