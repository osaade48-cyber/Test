FAQ

Fair questions. Straight answers.

Bundles and services

Which bundle is right for me?

It depends on your biggest problem. Look dated? Foundation. Not showing up on Google? Visibility. Missing calls? Reception. Need more work now? Growth. Not sure? Book a call and we'll work it out together.

What is Headstart?

An add-on that finds new council approvals near you, like new homes, extensions, granny flats and pools, and posts a letter from your business to the owner before they start looking for tradies. There's no setup fee when you add it to a bundle. See how it works.

Can I pick individual services instead of a bundle?

Yes. Mix and match any services and we'll build a custom plan on a call.

Do I need to know anything about marketing?

No. We set up and run the ads for you. You just approve them and turn up to quotes.

Will the ads look like my business?

Yes. They use your business name, your work and the suburbs you service. Do you use their photos? Confirm.

The calls

Will callers know it's AI?

It sounds natural and talks like your office would. If someone asks, it's honest that it's your virtual receptionist. Most callers just care that someone picked up.

What happens with emergencies?

You decide what counts as urgent. Urgent calls are flagged and texted to you straight away so you can call back fast.

What if a caller wants to talk to me?

It takes their details and texts you so you can call them back. Can it transfer live calls? Confirm.

Does it work after hours?

Yes. It can answer 24/7, or only when you choose.
