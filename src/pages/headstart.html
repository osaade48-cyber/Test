    Questions about Headstart

    Which trades is it for?

    Any trade whose work starts with a new build or a bigger project: builders, renovation and granny flat builders, landscapers, concreters, pool builders, fencers, excavators and demolition, plus electricians, plumbers and others who work on new builds.

    Where do the approvals come from?

    Public approval records published by the NSW Government on the NSW Planning Portal. We check them every day.

    Do you call or text the owners?

    No. We only send letters by post, addressed to the owner, with an easy way to opt out.

    How soon will I get work from it?

    Approved jobs often start weeks or months later, so Headstart builds a pipeline of upcoming work rather than instant calls. That's why there's a 3-month minimum.

    What if there are more than {{site.hsLetters}} jobs a month?

    Extra letters are ${{site.hsExtra}} each. Or we cap it at {{site.hsLetters}} so your bill never changes. Your choice.

    Is it cheaper with a bundle?

    Yes. Add Headstart to any bundle and there's no setup fee. You just pay the monthly price.

    Which areas do you cover?

    Anywhere in NSW. We set it up for the suburbs you actually work in.
