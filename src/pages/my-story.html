The problem

I run Avera Earthworx, an excavation business in Sydney. Most days I'm on the machine from first light. You can't answer the phone halfway through a site cut, and by the time I got to my voicemails at lunch, half of those people had already booked someone else.

I tried calling everyone back at the end of the day. I tried a call centre. What else did you try? What went wrong?

So I built my own

I set up an AI receptionist that answers like my office would. It knows the questions I'd ask: what suburb, what's the job, can a machine get down the side. It books the site visit and texts me the details. If someone hangs up, they get a text with an enquiry form.

How long did it take? What was the first job it won you?

Now I'm offering it to other tradies

Once it was working for me, other tradies I know started asking about it. So I made it into TradeBooked. Same setup, tuned for your trade, set up for you by someone who knows what life on the tools is like.
