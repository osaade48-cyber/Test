About Avera

Earthworks are the foundation for everything that follows. We treat them that way.

Who we are

Earthworks delivered with precision.

Avera Earthworx provides excavation, earthworks and earthmoving across residential and commercial sites in Sydney.

Our focus is simple: understand the site properly, plan the work clearly and complete each stage to a professional standard.

We specialise in tight access work — the backyards, side passages and close-quarters sites where bigger machines can’t go and care matters most.

Our approach

Every stage should set up the next one.

Levels, access, drainage, footing positions and final preparation all affect the trades that come after us — so we work with the end result in mind from the start.

01Before

Understand the ground

We look at access, levels, existing services, spoil movement and the finished result before excavation begins.

02During

Excavate with purpose

Each cut, trench and movement of material is carried out to suit the job — not simply to move dirt.

03After

Set up what follows

The site is left at the right level, condition and stage for drainage, footings, landscaping or the next trade.
