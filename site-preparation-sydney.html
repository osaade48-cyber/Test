Site Preparation

Clearing, levelling and preparing ground for the next stage of work.

Site preparation gets the ground ready for the next stage: clearing, levelling and shaping so builders, concreters and landscapers can get straight to work.

We prepare residential and commercial sites of all sizes, including tight blocks and backyards where access is limited.

When you might need it

  • A new build, extension or granny flat is about to start
  • A slab, shed or pad needs a level base
  • The site needs clearing before work begins

What’s included

  • Site clearing
  • Cut and fill
  • Levelling and grading
  • Slab and pad preparation
  • Removal of vegetation and debris
  • Spoil removal

What does site preparation include?

It usually covers clearing, levelling, cut and fill and removing unwanted material. Every site is different, so we confirm the scope on the free site visit.

Can you prepare a site for a slab?

Yes. We can excavate and level to the required heights ready for your concreter or builder.

Do you clear vegetation?

Yes, we can remove vegetation and debris as part of preparing the site.
