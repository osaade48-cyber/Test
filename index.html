What is tight access excavation?

It’s excavation on sites where a full-size machine can’t get in — side passages, narrow gates, backyards behind houses and areas close to fences, walls or finished surfaces. We use compact machinery and a careful, planned approach to get the work done without the damage or disruption bigger gear can cause.

How narrow an access can you work in?

It depends on the site and the job. Measure the narrowest point of your access (gate, side path or between structures) and include it in your enquiry — we’ll tell you straight away if we can get in.

Do you take away the spoil?

Yes. We can load, stockpile and remove spoil, rubble, concrete and green waste so the site is left clean and ready for the next trade.

What areas do you cover?

We’re based in Dural and work across Sydney. If you’re not sure whether we cover your suburb, just ask.

How do I get a quote?

Use the quote form, call or email. A short description of the work, rough size, access width and a few photos of the site are the most useful things to send.
